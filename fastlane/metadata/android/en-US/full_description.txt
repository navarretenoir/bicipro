Android cycling computer with offline maps and navigation for Chile (especially Santiago). Connects e-bike controllers and reads their codes, heart-rate monitors, speed/cadence and power sensors; controls any music player; records rides; works as a launcher. Android 5.0+. The Chile maps are downloaded by the app on first run (~1.2 GB). No ads, no trackers. (Interface in Spanish.)

Developed by Javiera Navarrete.
